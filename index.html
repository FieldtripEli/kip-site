Your money, kept close.

KIP is a personal budgeting app for Windows and Android. It tracks spending, matches bank lines to invoices and receipts, and keeps budgets as friendly guidelines rather than hard limits.

On your devices

Your data lives in an encrypted database on your own PC and phone, locked with your PIN.

Synced privately

Devices keep in step through a folder in your own Google Drive, encrypted so Google can't read it.

No one else

No KIP server, no ads, no analytics and no selling of data. Ever.

Who it's for

KIP is a small personal project, made for one household in Aotearoa New Zealand. It isn't sold or listed in any app store.

Google sign-in

KIP asks Google for one thing only: Access to the files KIP itself creates in your Drive (the drive.file permission). It can't see your other files, your email or your contacts. Read the privacy policy for the details.
